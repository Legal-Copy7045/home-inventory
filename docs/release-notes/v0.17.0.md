Estimate what everything is worth, in one go.

**Updating:** update the App. No database changes.

## New
- **Estimate values with AI** on the **Inventory report** page. Choose:
  - **Items without a price**: fill the gaps in your insurance list;
  - **Items in a category**: for example all your Tools or Electronics;
  - **Everything**.

  Before you start it tells you how many items and AI requests that is (the same product in several places is estimated once, and anything estimated in the last 30 days is skipped unless you tick *Include items estimated in the last 30 days*). It runs in the background with a progress bar, and you can stop it. Each item keeps its estimate (replacement price and second-hand range, dated and marked as AI). Prices you entered always come first. Only administrators can start it, because it may cost money with a paid AI provider.
- The report shows the AI estimate (≈) for items without a price, and a total.
