Photograph a whole area, get voice answers on your phone, and better name matching for voice.

**Updating:**
1. Update the App and the integration in HACS, then restart Home Assistant.
2. There are no database changes, and nothing to change in Alexa or Assist.
3. To get answers on your phone, turn the option on under Settings → Devices & services → Home Inventory → Configure.

## New
- **Photo inventory of a whole area.** On a location, choose **Photo inventory** and photograph a whole rack, bench, pegboard or corner.
  - The AI works out the units and shelves and recognises each thing.
  - It puts each thing on the right shelf, and turns labelled boxes into containers named after their label.
  - You check the layout and the list before anything is saved.
  - Existing shelves and boxes are matched, so photographing the same area again doesn't duplicate them.
- **Voice answers on your phone.** The option "Also send voice answers to your phone" can be off, Alexa only, or all voice assistants.
  - It sends the answer, with where the thing is, through the notify action you pick, for example the companion app's `mobile_app_<phone>`.
  - Only final answers are sent, not "which one did you mean?" questions.

## Fixed
- **Voice name matching:**
  - "Glass", "Compass", "Class" and "Status" are no longer cut to "Glas", "Compas", "Clas" or "Statu".
  - Plurals are matched with explicit rules ("batteries" = "battery", "shelves" = "shelf"), and words that are only ever plural ("Safety glasses", "Pliers") keep their meaning.
  - Sizes and model numbers match however they're written ("50 ft" = "50-foot", "HRX-217" = "HRX 217"), while different sizes stay different.
