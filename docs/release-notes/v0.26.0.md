Finding things: photos with voice answers, "what's in box 5?", "which shelf is this?", and fixing names Alexa doesn't know.

**Updating:** update the App and the Home Inventory integration (HACS), then restart Home Assistant. For "what's in…", also copy the new `deploy/assist/custom_sentences/en/home_inventory.yaml` and update the Alexa skill (interaction model and `lambda_function.py`, see *Updating the skill* in `docs/voice.md`). No database changes.

## New
- **Photos with voice answers:** phone notifications for an answer show the item's photo.
- **"What's in box 5?" / "What's on the top shelf?"** by voice.
- **Which shelf is this?** on the Scan page: identify a place from a photo and see what's missing from it.
- **Admin → Voice history → Names it didn't recognise:** add what people say as another name for the right item in one tap.
