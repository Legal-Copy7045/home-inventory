Fixes reading a manual failing with "Load failed" (Safari, Home Assistant app) or "Failed to fetch" (Edge, Chrome).

**Updating:** update the App. The integration doesn't change.

## Fixed
- Reading a long manual can take a few minutes, and browsers and proxies gave up waiting. It now runs in the background:
  - the app shows *Reading the manual…* and picks up the result when it's ready;
  - you can close the screen and come back later, and the result waits on the item;
  - tapping **Read it** again while a reading is running doesn't start a second one;
  - a reading interrupted by an App restart says so, instead of waiting forever.
