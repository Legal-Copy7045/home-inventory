Home Assistant sensors on your machines, parts reserved for repairs, and one list of what to buy.

**Updating:** update the App (the integration only changes its version number). The App runs one automatic, additive database migration (entity links and reservations).

## New
- **Link entities to equipment**: runtime sensors and counters feed maintenance; battery, energy, power and on/off entities are shown on the equipment page. Checked and confirmed before linking.
- **Parts reserved for a job**: available, reserved and used are kept apart; using is a separate step.
- **What to buy** on Restock & Shopping: maintenance, minimum stock and reservations added up per part, by shop.
- **Quick actions** on equipment pages, and NFC tags on equipment open its page.
