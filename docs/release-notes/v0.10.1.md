Voice now asks "which one did you mean?" when a question matches several different things.

**Updating:** update the App and the integration (HACS), then restart Home Assistant. No database changes.

## Changed
- "Where is the hose?" used to read out every match in one long sentence. Now it asks: *"I found 3 things for hose: Kink Control Plus Water Hose 50ft, Siphon pump hose and Siphon pump with hose and funnel. Which one did you mean?"* Reply "the water hose one", "the one with the funnel" or "the second one".
- Several records of the same thing are answered together ("Battery is in A, and in B").
- Alexa and Assist voice devices keep listening for your reply (for two minutes).

## Update your voice setup
- **Alexa:**
  1. In the developer console, paste the new [`deploy/alexa/interaction-model.json`](https://github.com/Legal-Copy7045/home-inventory/blob/main/deploy/alexa/interaction-model.json) into the JSON Editor, then Save and **Build skill**.
  2. In the Code tab, replace `lambda_function.py` with the new [`deploy/alexa/lambda_function.py`](https://github.com/Legal-Copy7045/home-inventory/blob/main/deploy/alexa/lambda_function.py), keeping your `HA_URL`, `HA_TOKEN` and `SKILL_ID`.
  3. Deploy.
- **Assist:** add the `HomeInventoryChoose` lines from [docs/voice.md](https://github.com/Legal-Copy7045/home-inventory/blob/main/docs/voice.md) to your `custom_sentences` file and restart.

Tip: if the same item shows up twice under different names, **Items → Possible duplicates** merges them.
