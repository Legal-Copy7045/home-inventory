A big one: work with many items at once, never lose a quick photo, an insurance-ready report, Home Assistant sensors for operating hours, duplicate clean-up, and asking "where is…?" by voice.

**Before updating:** take a Home Assistant backup. This release includes small database changes, applied automatically after a snapshot. Then update the companion integration in HACS and restart Home Assistant (needed for voice). If you skipped earlier releases, also read the [v0.9.0](https://github.com/Legal-Copy7045/home-inventory/blob/main/docs/release-notes/v0.9.0.md), [v0.8.0](https://github.com/Legal-Copy7045/home-inventory/blob/main/docs/release-notes/v0.8.0.md) and [v0.7.1](https://github.com/Legal-Copy7045/home-inventory/blob/main/docs/release-notes/v0.7.1.md) notes; v0.7.1 has security fixes.

## Fixed
- **Items page showed at most 300 items.** It now pages through everything (**Show more**), and **Select all** selects every item matching the filter.
- **A lent-out item could be set to "In storage" with its loan still open.** Now refused; mark the loan returned instead.
- **Photo inventory review could open empty** after the AI finished.

## Working with many items
- **Select** on Items, container contents, a location's loose items, search results and To finish.
- Then **Move**, **Edit**, **Lend**, **Add to kit** or **Delete** together.
- **Undo** straight after deleting, and a **Recently deleted** page to restore several at once.
- **Sort by location** groups items under their place.

## Quick photos and scanning
- **Quick photos are saved on your phone first.** Lost signal or closed the app? They upload next time you open Home Inventory. A banner shows how many are waiting.
- **Scan → Take one out:** every barcode scanned uses one from stock. Restock alerts update straight away.
- **Faster loading on a weak signal:** the app itself is cached on your phone (never your inventory data).

## Reports and paperwork
- **Inventory Report:** everything grouped by room, with photos, serial numbers, purchase details, value and receipts. Print or save as PDF, or download CSV or Excel.
- **Read receipts with AI:** add a receipt photo and copy its date, price, shop and warranty to the item.
- A ready-made Home Assistant automation for warranty-ending reminders (in the docs).

## Equipment and clean-up
- **Operating hours from Home Assistant:** link a runtime sensor or counter to a mower, generator or saw. It's read every 15 minutes and maintenance due dates follow real use.
- **Possible duplicates:** find things recorded twice and merge them in one tap.
- **Photo inventory:** each new item gets its own picture cut from the shelf photo. Things not seen can be marked missing instead of deleted.
- **NFC tags: Move to…** points a tag at a different item, box, place or kit.

## Voice
- **"Where is the hedge trimmer?"** with Home Assistant Assist, Siri (through a Shortcut) or an Alexa custom skill.
- **Asks when it's unclear:** "I found 3 things for hose: … Which one did you mean?" Reply "the water hose one" or "the second one". Setup: [docs/voice.md](https://github.com/Legal-Copy7045/home-inventory/blob/main/docs/voice.md).
