Maintenance reminders with parts, a seasonal checklist, wide-area photo inventories, warranty claim packs, value estimates and more voice questions.

**Updating:** update the App and the Home Inventory integration (HACS). The database is updated automatically when the App starts (one new column; a snapshot is taken first). For the new voice questions, update your custom sentences file and, if you use the Alexa skill, its interaction model and Lambda code (see `docs/voice.md`).

## New
- **Maintenance reminders.** Equipment that becomes due for service sends one reminder per service: what's due, and whether the parts are in stock. Turn on **Send maintenance reminders to your phone** in the integration's options to get them as notifications. In automations, use the `home_inventory_maintenance_due` event.
- **Missing parts go on the shopping list.** Parts a due job needs that aren't in stock are added to **Restock**, noting the job they're for. Turn this off under **Settings** if you'd rather add them yourself.
- **This season.** The Maintenance page's new tab lists jobs dated in the next three months and suggests seasonal ones your equipment doesn't have, such as winterising the mower. One tap adds it as a yearly task. Southern hemisphere? Choose it under **Settings → Seasons**.
- **Wide area (2-4 photos).** Photograph a long wall or bench left to right in a few photos. They're joined into one panorama for the photo inventory.
- **Print labels for new boxes** right after a photo inventory adds them.
- **Warranty claim pack.** One download with a summary of the item (serial number, purchase and warranty details) plus its receipts, documents and photos.
- **Value estimates.** **Estimate with AI** on an item gives a rough replacement price and second-hand range. The inventory report totals estimates for items without a price.
- **More voice questions.** "What maintenance is due?", "What's running low?", "What's lent out?"
