Tidier item pages: Maintenance only where it belongs.

**Updating:** update the App. No database changes.

## Changed
- Equipment is the only category with maintenance by default. The **Maintenance** section only shows on equipment, and **Track maintenance** (at the foot of *Details*) only appears on items in the **Equipment** category. Set an item's category to Equipment to track it; items you already track keep their section. The AI now suggests Equipment for mowers, generators and other powered machines.
- To start tracking, tap **Track maintenance**. You can then read its manual, which sets up the service schedule and parts, or pick the type of equipment.
- Manual documents no longer have their own "Read for the service schedule and parts" button. It repeated **Read the manual** and didn't fit on phones.
