Ask where things go back, and a shorter menu.

**Updating:** update the App and the Home Inventory integration (HACS). No database changes. For the new voice question, copy the new `deploy/assist/custom_sentences/en/home_inventory.yaml` to `custom_sentences/en/` in your Home Assistant configuration folder, then reload conversation (or restart). The Alexa skill doesn't change.

## New
- **"Where does the hammer go?"** Assist (and Siri through Assist) answers with where something belongs, for putting it back, and says where it is now if it's out. Also: "Tell me where to put the engine oil", "Where should I put my drill back?", "Where does the jigsaw belong?".

## Changed
- **Menu sections fold away.** Tap Inventory, Reports or Admin to open or close it; it remembers, and the section you're in stays open.
