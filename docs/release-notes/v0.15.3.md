Reliability fixes from the code review, and faster item lists.

**Updating:** update the App. No database changes.

## Fixed
- A meter photo chosen from your library uses the date the photo was taken (from the photo itself). Before, it used the file's modified date, which can be the day it was copied or shared.
- If AI is too busy to read a document you upload, the document shows it with **Try again** instead of silently not being read.
- Reading manuals and documents runs at most three at a time; the rest wait their turn, so a batch of uploads can't overload the App or your AI provider.
- Search, photo inventory matching and location search use the same rule for plurals.

## Faster
- Item lists, search results and the dashboard fetch photos, loans and owners in a few queries rather than several per item.
