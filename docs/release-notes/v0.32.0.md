Equipment gets its own section: a page per machine with its manufacturer-manual checklist, parts in stock and to buy, and maintenance reports.

**Updating:** update the App (the integration only changes its version number). The App runs one automatic, additive database migration. Your equipment and tasks are kept; existing tasks are marked "added by hand" (their origin wasn't recorded before) and grouped by their intervals.

## New
- **Equipment** section: list, filters and a page per machine (status, checklist, parts, details, history).
- **Manage as equipment** / **Make it an ordinary item**, without losing anything.
- **Manual-derived checklist** with the source file, section and page of every task; generic schedules are labelled as such.
- **Model check** before a manual's schedule is used; **your edits are kept** when a manual is read again.
- **Printable checklist** and **Maintenance reports** (overdue, upcoming, history, all equipment).
