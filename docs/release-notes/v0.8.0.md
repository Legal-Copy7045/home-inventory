Quick photos is now its own feature. Photograph everything on several shelves first, then say which shelf each group is on.

**Updating:** automatic; no database changes. If you skipped v0.7.1, this release also includes its security fixes. See the [v0.7.1 notes](https://github.com/Legal-Copy7045/home-inventory/blob/main/docs/release-notes/v0.7.1.md).

## New

### Quick photos without a place
- **Quick photos** is now in the main menu and on the dashboard.
- Take one photo per item. Tap **Next shelf** each time you move to another shelf, box or drawer.
- Photos are saved straight away as "Item to identify". AI suggestions still work if AI is set up.

### To sort
- Shows the photos taken without a place, grouped shelf by shelf, newest first.
- Tap **Put all in…** and choose a location or a container. Every photo in the group moves there at once.
- Untick a photo that belongs somewhere else and put it away separately.
- The dashboard shows how many photos are waiting to be sorted.

Details are still added from **To finish**, before or after sorting. Quick photos from inside a container or location work as before.
