Categories become a list you manage, not free text.

**Updating:** update the App. No database changes; your existing categories stay. Tidy duplicates (e.g. "Kitchenware" and "Kitchen and dining") under **Admin → Categories → Merge**.

## New
- **Admin → Categories:** add, rename, merge and delete categories, and give them "also known as" names. Merging moves the items and keeps the old name as an also-known-as, so future suggestions with it land in the right category.

## Changed
- Items choose their category from the list (item form, bulk edit, photo inventory review). AI and barcode suggestions are matched to your categories, or left blank with the suggestion shown.
- Only administrators can add or change categories.
