Photograph the hour meter, see how much your equipment is used, and get dates for hours-based maintenance.

**Updating:** update the App (and the integration in HACS to keep versions together). The database upgrade adds one column.

## New
- **Photograph the hour meter.** On equipment, **Log hours → Photograph the hour meter**. AI reads the number (including the tenths) and you check it. The photo is kept with the reading.
- **Readings for any day.** Choose the date of a reading; photos from your gallery use the day they were taken.
- **Use over time.** A chart of hours against date, with your average hours a week.
- **Dates for hours-based work.** Tasks such as "oil every 50 hours" show roughly when they'll be due at your rate of use, and appear on the Maintenance page in time.

## Notes
- The AI never guesses: if the meter can't be read clearly, it says so and you type the number.
- A reading lower than an earlier one needs **Correction** ticked (for a replaced or reset meter).
