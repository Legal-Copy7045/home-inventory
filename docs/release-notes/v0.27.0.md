Keeping the inventory honest: shelf check-ups and duplicates spotted by their photos.

**Updating:** update the App. No database changes.

## New
- **Shelf check-up** on the dashboard: the place that most needs a fresh photo inventory, one tap to check it. A monthly `home_inventory_checkup_due` event lets a Home Assistant automation remind you.
- **Possible duplicates** now also spots products whose photos look the same, even with different names.

### Example reminder automation
```yaml
automation:
  - alias: Shelf check-up reminder
    triggers:
      - trigger: event
        event_type: home_inventory_checkup_due
    actions:
      - action: notify.mobile_app_your_phone
        data:
          title: Shelf check-up
          message: "Time to photograph {{ trigger.event.data.path }} ({{ trigger.event.data.things }} things)."
```
