Take things out, put them back: by scan or by photo.

**Updating:** update the App. No database changes.

## New
- **Take it / put away.** Tap **Take it** on an item (or after scanning its barcode) and it's marked as with you. Later, scan the label where you put it: back in its own place it's simply put away; somewhere new, choose **Move it here** or **Just this once**.
- **What is this? Identify by photo** on the Scan page (needs AI): the AI names the thing in the photo and lists your matching items, so you can take it, take one out, or put it away without a barcode. The photo isn't stored.
