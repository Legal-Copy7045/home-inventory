Things the AI can't identify in a photo inventory are kept for you to finish, earlier photos can be looked at again, and every photo button lets you pick from your library.

**Updating:** update the App. No database changes.

## Changed
- A photo of a shelf, rack or whole area now lists **everything** the AI can see.
  - Things it can't identify get a plain description (e.g. "Black hard case") and the badge **Unidentified – to finish**.
  - When you confirm, they're saved with their own cut-out photo on the **To finish** list, so you can identify them later.
  - If you know what one is during the review, rename it and untick the badge.
- **Last photo again:** on a location or container, have the AI look at the last photo inventory photo again, with no need to retake it. Things already recorded are recognised; only new things are offered.
- **Pick from your library:** every photo button now lets you choose a photo from your library as well as take one.
