Home Inventory grows from a household inventory into equipment management: maintenance schedules, repairs you do for other people, accessories and kits, NFC tags and restocking. This release also includes a round of security hardening.

**Before updating:** take a Home Assistant backup. Updating is otherwise automatic. Your data and roles are kept, and every database change is preceded by an automatic snapshot. Then update the companion integration in HACS and restart Home Assistant to get the new sensors.

## New

### Maintenance
Open an item such as a mower, generator or saw, and tap **Track maintenance**.
- Tasks repeat by date, operating hours or uses, whichever comes first.
- Log hours (estimates are fine) and record service with parts, costs, who did it, and photos or receipts. The next due date is worked out for you.
- Ready-made starting points for mowers, trimmers, chainsaws, blowers, generators, pressure washers and snow blowers.
- A **Maintenance** page shows what's overdue, due in 7/30/90 days, or has no records.

### Customers and repair jobs
For equipment you fix for other people.
- Their equipment is clearly marked and never counts as yours.
- Repair jobs run from received to returned, with condition, diagnosis, parts and labor, photos, multiple visits, and a printable work order.

### Related items and kits
- Link accessories, components and compatible parts. Each stays its own item in its own place, and searching for one shows the others.
- Kits show which members are with the kit, stored elsewhere, on loan or missing.

### NFC tags
- Tap a tag to open an item, box, shelf or kit, or to jump straight to *Log hours*, *Log service*, *Move* and more.
- **Android:** writes tags in the app.
- **iPhone:** copy the link into an NFC writer app.
- The NFC guide is in `docs/nfc.md`.

### Restocking and shopping list
- Set a minimum and a restock level for each item, plus where to buy it.
- The shopping list is grouped by shop. Amazon gets a confirm-to-add-to-cart link; Lowe's and Tractor Supply open the exact product page.
- Nothing is ever bought or added for you.

### Home Assistant
- New sensors: maintenance overdue and due soon, repairs in progress and awaiting pickup, supplies below minimum, shopping list items.
- New events: maintenance completed, repair status, tag scanned, shopping list updated.

### Better photos on phones
- Item photos show whole and centred.
- Tapping a thumbnail switches the photo.
- Tapping the photo opens a full-screen viewer with swipe and pinch-to-zoom.

## Security
- **No automatic administrator.** If Home Assistant can't say who the admins are, the App prints a one-time setup code in its log instead of promoting the first visitor.
- **Restore** checks backup archives fully before touching your data, and puts everything back if it fails.
- **Product-link import** can't be redirected to devices on your home network (DNS rebinding).
- **No `/media` access.** The App no longer mounts Home Assistant's `/media` folder.
- **Limits:** upload size limits and per-person rate limits on AI and online lookups.
- **0.7.1:**
  - Read-only users can no longer reach customer repair photos or documents, and no longer see customer equipment at all.
  - A household purchase can't be added to a customer's holding.
  - Someone made read-only in the app can't use the setup code to become administrator.
- **CI:** dependency audits, a secret scan and a container image scan run on every change.
