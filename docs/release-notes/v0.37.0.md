A shopping list you can shop from, and spark plugs that count. No database changes; update the App (the integration only changes its version number).

## New
- Shopping list entries show the maker, part number, shop product number and barcode (tap to copy) and a link to the product page.
- Add anything to the shopping list by hand, including things not in your inventory yet.
- **Bought** asks where it goes: added to a place you already keep it, somewhere new, or just recorded.
- **Use a standard I have…** on an equipment page's parts list links a specific part from the manual to a standard you keep, so your stock counts.
