Parts edited where you see them, put-away that defaults to home, merging duplicates with their quantities, and fixes to the menu and the camera. No database changes; update the App (the integration only changes its version number).

## New
- Edit a maintenance task's parts and consumables straight from the equipment page (Parts and consumables → Edit, the checklist's "Needs" line, or **+ Add parts**), and when adding or editing a task.
- Merging possible duplicates asks whether to add the quantities together, treat them as the same thing recorded twice, or keep them separate.
- Tap a photo while selecting items to see it full size.

## Changed
- **Put away** returns an item to where it lives; going somewhere else is a **Move**.

## Fixed
- Menu sections now always open and close (the current page's section couldn't be closed before).
- The Quick photos shutter is no longer hidden behind the bottom bar on phones.
